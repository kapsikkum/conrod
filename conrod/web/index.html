    Your photographs are not touched by either of these — nothing Conrod decides is ever written to a file until you press Write XMP. Your settings and entry list are kept.

    Detections and identifications throws away every car Conrod found and everything it read off them, and keeps the albums indexed so a re-run does not have to read your files again.
    Everything forgets the albums as well.

    Setup

    The detectors are small and fetch themselves. The vision model is about 6 GB and runs under Ollama — it is what identifies make, model, colour and team. Without it the app still reads plates, numbers and text.
